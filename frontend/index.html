How it works

Simple steps, smart results

Whether you're listing spare capacity or searching for it, the flow stays the same on both sides of the exchange.

1

Search or list

Seekers search by type, location, date and budget. Providers list spare capacity with price and conditions.

2

Compare matches

Results are ranked by price fit, distance, rating, availability and urgency — with the reasoning shown, not hidden.

3

Request & negotiate

Send a request, counter-offer if needed, and track status from pending through to confirmed.

4

Confirm & review

Confirming locks the time slot so it can't be double-booked. Both sides rate the exchange afterward.
